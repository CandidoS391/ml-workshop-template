# Report guide

Adapt this outline to your project and audience. Agree on length and format with
your team; combine or remove sections as needed.

1. **Question and motivation:** What did you explore, and why?
2. **Data and approach:** What did you use, what choices did you make, and why?
3. **Evaluation and findings:** How did you assess the work, and what did you observe?
4. **Limitations and responsible use:** What uncertainties, biases, or risks affect the conclusions?
5. **Reflection and next steps:** What did you learn, and what would you try next?
6. **References:** Credit data, methods, code, and other sources used.

Include only figures or tables that help explain the findings. Link to supporting
work and describe how others can reproduce it where practical.
